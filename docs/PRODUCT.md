# Product contract

AirWise Schools supports an administrator repairing school activities after
reviewing evidence and confirming restrictions. The eventual workflow is evidence,
confirmed restriction, repaired plan, independent validation, approval, teacher
execution report and operational review.

This user-scoped Packet A delivers only a local foundation: health API, synthetic
school fixture, native solver smoke test, read-only React skeleton and ZIP spike.
The attached blueprint is planning context; the user's explicit exclusions and
deployment order govern this implementation.

One fictional NCR school, six sections, aggregate sizes only. All fixture data is
SYNTHETIC, with no live pollution values, child identities or medical records.
No claim of legal restrictions, health benefits or working schedule repair.

Packet B must add the weekly eight-period fixture, baseline, deterministic repair
and independent validator before any plan can be called valid. Approval,
permissions, persistence, teacher reports and evidence adapters are later work.
Bedrock, SQS, Cognito and DynamoDB are explicitly deferred here.
